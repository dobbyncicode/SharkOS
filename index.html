v2.0.0
28 September 2026  ·  "2nd Anniversary Edition"
Breaking

Taskbar deleted. There is a menu bar now, at the top. The dock moved to the bottom. No migration path, no deprecation notice, no apology.

Entire visual layer replaced with a glassmorphism system: real backdrop-filter blur, saturated fills, hairline borders. Every surface. The old build was a reskin; this one is a rewrite.

Added

Our Story: a three-act ASCII story. 14 scenes, 3 endings, branchable, plays start to finish without stalling.

A "What's New" button in the menu bar, deliberately near-invisible, for when you dismiss this and immediately regret it.

Clock widget. Dock magnification that lerps instead of snapping.

Fixed

The story froze. Twice. Two next pointers referenced act2 / act3, which are array names and not scene ids, so the lookup returned -1 and the engine silently returned. Both are fixed.

Added resolveNext(): an unresolvable next now falls back to the next scene in array order and logs a warning, so a bad link can never freeze the UI again.

Backslashes in the ASCII art were being silently consumed by the JS template literal, so the guinea pigs rendered with no legs. The art now matches the source.

Leftover debug art ("SOMETHING IS WRONG") removed from the logout scene.

Writing

All 14 scenes rebuilt to a uniform 25-column frame with aligned box interiors.

She has agency now. She opens the conversation, she demands the confession, she sets the date, she pushes to meet, she picks the staycation. Previously she just received things.

Point of view is strict: "you" is her, "he" is him. Cut every third-person slip ("the two of them", "we know", "She finished nursing").

Consistency pass against the real timeline. Removed a breadwinner who never existed, a hallway in a building he was never physically in, and a line that flatly contradicted the next one.

Cut the legal disclaimer from the ending. It was deflating the landing it sat under.

Untouched

The five reasons. The shark game. The four songs. The puzzle gate: still "1 + 2 = ?", still "sharkos". The playlist is still curated.
